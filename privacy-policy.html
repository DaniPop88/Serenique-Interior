Privacy Policy

At Serenique Interior Haven, your privacy and trust are important to us. We are committed to safeguarding your personal information and being transparent about how we handle your data.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our products and services, process orders, and communicate with you. This typically includes your name, contact number, email address, delivery details, and order preferences.

How We Use Your Data

  • To process and fulfill your orders and service requests.
  • To contact you regarding order confirmation, product availability, and scheduling delivery or installation.
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • To improve our services and enhance your experience.

Protection & Sharing of Information

We treat your personal data with care and do not sell or share it with third parties except when required to fulfill your order, such as sharing delivery details with our trusted logistics partners. We use secure systems to store and manage your information, accessible only to authorized staff.

Your Choices & Rights

  • You may review and update your personal information by contacting us.
  • We respect requests to limit or delete your data, unless retention is required by law or for business records.
  • For a full copy of our detailed privacy policy, please email hello@sereniquehaven.site.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any revisions will be posted on this page and will take effect immediately.
