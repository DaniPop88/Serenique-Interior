A house becomes home

Serenique Interior Haven was founded by Serena Marie Asistio Anonuevo after years of helping friends and family create beautiful, functional living spaces. She started it after seeing how the right furnishings could transform a house into a true home.

We source from local makers and trusted suppliers, combining quality, durability and affordability. Our mission is to make elegant, practical interiors accessible to Filipino families through warm hospitality, honest service and expert advice.
