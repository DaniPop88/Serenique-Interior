About Serenique Interior Haven
Serenique Interior Haven was founded by Serena Marie Asistio Anonuevo after years of helping friends and family create beautiful, functional living spaces. Our mission is to bring thoughtfully chosen décor, furniture, and lifestyle essentials to Filipino homes.
Our Story
Serena Marie started Serenique after seeing how the right furnishings could transform a house into a true home. We source from local makers and trusted suppliers—combining quality, durability, and affordability.
Our Mission
To make elegant, practical interiors accessible to Filipino families through warm hospitality, honest service, and expert advice. We help you design your space—and define your style.
Our Values
Local craft first · Honest pricing · Customer care · Lasting relationships · Pride in Filipino hospitality.
